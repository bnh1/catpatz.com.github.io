Riding the swans at bakken

I tried to draw our beautiful new niece Isabella today, but I struggled trying to get her remotely adequately cute. My attempts so far, Ben tells me, look more like aliens or corpses than little bundles of joy. So, I have tabled this for another day and drawn us on the swan ride at Bakken amusement park outside Copenhagen (in the kings hunting grounds). We biked there with my parents and sister a couple weekends ago. The swan ride is not really a ride but more of a paddleboat in the air. We also rode the log flume and what may be the oldest roller coaster in the world. And ate smorrebrod.

I drew this comic using brushes and not sketchbook pro. Brushes has the benefit of supposedly being able to export in higher resolution, but no local fill feature.
